We use measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encryption or hashing of credentials, access controls limited to staff who need the data, logging and monitoring, and regular reviews. **Service providers assume responsibility for the security of data they process on our instructions in accordance with their contractual obligations.**

### 10. Your rights

Under the DPPA you may:

- Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- Delete your data where there is no lawful reason for us to keep it;
- Object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing and automated decisions with significant effects;
- Withdraw consent at any time;
- Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO), under the National Information Technology Authority – Uganda (NITA-U), if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise a right, use Settings > Privacy in the App or email privacy@medveris.ug. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within 30 days or the period set by law.

**Location data right:** If precise location was collected to help you find a provider, you may withdraw location consent at any time, though the provider-search feature will not function without it.

### 11. Children

The App is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

### 12. Cookies and local storage

The App uses essential cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and protect against abuse. We use analytics or non-essential storage only if you opt in. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy.

### 13. Third-party links

The App may link to third-party sites we do not control. Their privacy practices are their own; please read their policies.

### 14. Changes to this policy
